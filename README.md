# ⚡ Pokémon EDA
 
**Dataset:** [The Complete Pokémon Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rounakbanik/pokemon) — ~800 Pokémon with battle attributes, type, generation, and legendary status.
 
---
 
## Structure
 
```
pokemon-eda/
├── data/
│   ├── pokemon.csv
│   └── pokemon_clean.csv
├── notebooks/
│   ├── 01_cleaning.ipynb
│   └── 02_analysis.ipynb
└── README.md
```
 
---
 
## Steps
 
**01_cleaning.ipynb** — column selection, null treatment, and export of the clean dataset.
 
**02_analysis.ipynb** — measures of position, dispersion, and shape; histogram, bar chart, scatter plot, boxplot, and line chart.
 
---
 
## Key findings
 
- Dragon has the highest average total power, but does not lead in any single attribute, it is consistently strong across all dimensions.
- No type dominates everything: each type has its own specialty, indicating the game is well balanced.
- Attack has the highest dispersion among Pokémon; HP is the most homogeneous, suggesting the game equalizes survival but differentiates offensive power by type.
- Legendary Pokémon cluster at the extremes of the scatter plot (Attack × Defense), but the absolute highest defense values belong to common Pokémon.
- Newer generations are not linearly stronger: generations 4 and 7 stand out from the rest in average total power.
